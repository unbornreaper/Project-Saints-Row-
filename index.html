❓ Help

🏆 Will this mess up my trophies?

No. Your trophies are kept on your PlayStation account, not in the save file. WBWHO only changes the save file, so it can't delete or change trophies you already have.

Two things to know:

  • If you give yourself loads of money or unlock everything, the game might hand you a trophy for it later. If you only want trophies you earned yourself, don't max out things that have trophies.
  • When you put the save back on your PlayStation, sign it to your own account (Save Wizard calls this "Re-sign"). That keeps everything tied to you.
🔒 My save is "locked". What do I do?

PlayStation locks (encrypts) every save so you can't open it on a computer. You need a tool to unlock it first. WBWHO can't do that part.

  • Save Wizard for PS4 MAX (costs money, works on any PS4 or PS5): use Advanced Mode to Decrypt (get the file out), then Replace (put your edited file back).
  • Apollo Save Tool (free, only on a jailbroken PS4): use "Decrypt save files", then "Import decrypted file".

⚠️ PS5 note: Saves from the PS5 version of a game are much harder to get off the console and usually can't be unlocked right now. The PS4 version of the game (which also plays on PS5) is the one that works.

🪜 The whole thing, step by step
  1. On PlayStation: Settings → Saved Data → Copy to USB.
  2. On your computer: unlock the save (see the 🔒 part above).
  3. Open the unlocked file in WBWHO.
  4. Tap 🛟 Download a backup copy. Keep it safe!
  5. Change what you want on 🎮 Edit, or find new things on 🔎 Find.
  6. Tap 💾 Save. You get a file with the same name. Don't rename it!
  7. Use your unlock tool to put it back (Replace / Import) and re-sign it to your account.
  8. Copy it back from the USB to your PlayStation, then load the game. 🎉
💰 How do I find my money (or anything else)?
  1. In the game, look at how much money you have. Example: 12500.
  2. On 🔎 Find, type 12500 and tap Find it!
  3. If it finds one spot, that's it! Tap ⭐ Make a button.
  4. If it finds lots of spots, go back in the game, spend or earn some money, save, unlock that new save, then use "Load my newer save" and type the new number. Do this until only one spot is left.

Try it with the two fake saves first. It's easy once you've done it once!

🚗🔫👕 Cars, guns, outfits & missions

These are usually stored as on/off switches, not numbers. The easiest way to find them:

  1. Save the game (this is BEFORE).
  2. Unlock one thing: buy a gun, finish a mission, get an outfit.
  3. Save again (this is AFTER).
  4. Put both saves in 🆚 Compare. It shows what changed. Make a button from it, or copy the change into your save.

You can also search for a name with 🔤 Find a word, then look at the boxes next to it.

📖 Word list (what do these mean?)
  • Save file: the file where the game keeps your progress.
  • Locked / encrypted: scrambled so only the PlayStation can read it.
  • Unlocked / decrypted: unscrambled, so WBWHO can read it.
  • Re-sign: stamping the save with your account so your PlayStation accepts it.
  • Backup: a safe copy of your save, just in case.
  • Spot #: where something is inside the file, like a page number in a book.
  • Cheat list: all your WBWHO buttons saved in one small file.
  • Check code: a number the game uses to tell if a save was changed. WBWHO fixes it for you when it can.
🛟 Safety tips
  • Always keep a backup.
  • Change a few things, test in the game, then change more. It makes problems easy to find.
  • Don't make numbers crazy big. Big but normal (like 999,999,999 money) is safest.
  • Play modded saves on your own, not online with other people.
  • If the game says the save is broken, put your backup back. No harm done.
