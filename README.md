# ⭐ WBWHO Save Editor

A simple save editor for Saints Row on PS4/PS5. It works on your computer **and** your phone.

- 💰 Money, XP, level, health, ammo: anything with a number
- 🔫🚗👕🎬 Guns, cars, outfits, missions: on/off switches
- 🔎 Finds where things are hidden in **your** save
- 🆚 Compares two saves to see what a mission or unlock changed
- 🧾 Shows the raw inside of the file, if you want to poke around
- ↩️ Undo, 🛟 backups, and automatic check-code fixing
- 🏆 Never touches your trophies (they live on your PlayStation account, not in the save)

## How to open it

**Easiest:** double-click `index.html`. It opens in your web browser. No internet needed.

**As an app:** once it's online (see below), open it in Chrome or Edge and click **Install**. On a phone, use **Add to Home Screen**.

## Put it online (so you can use it on your phone)

1. Merge this branch into `main`.
2. On GitHub, go to **Settings → Pages**. Under **Source**, pick **GitHub Actions**.
3. Wait about a minute. Your link will be `https://<your-name>.github.io/Project-Saints-Row-/`.

## Important

PlayStation **locks** every save. Before WBWHO can read one, you have to **unlock** it with **Save Wizard for PS4 MAX** or **Apollo Save Tool**. Afterwards, the same tool puts the edited save back and re-signs it to your account. Open **❓ Help** inside the app for step-by-step instructions in simple words.

Nobody has published how Saints Row's save is laid out yet. So the first time, you find things with **🔎 Find** or **🆚 Compare**. WBWHO turns what you find into one-tap buttons and remembers them.

Always keep a backup. 🛟
